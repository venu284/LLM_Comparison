## 1. Setup

- **Data.** As in P0.5: CodeRouterBench at `e567d89b` and route-anything-data at `bd361209`.
  `pipeline/venv/bin/python scripts/fetch_data.py --verify-only` prints `fetched: 0, verified: 187`.
- **Script.** `router/experiments/p0_6_cellbest_bound.py`. It imports P0.5's functions and does not
  change their definitions. It writes every number below to
  `router/experiments/out/p0_6_cellbest_bound.json` (JSON paths are given as `key.key`).
  Regenerate from the repo root with:

  ```
  pipeline/venv/bin/python router/experiments/p0_6_cellbest_bound.py
  ```

  The full run took 54.7 s. It stops if P0.5 does not reproduce, if the prompt join is incomplete,
  if true-cell CellBest differs from P0.5's CellBest, or if the fixed-ladder counts differ from
  P0.5's S3.2 table. `tests/router/test_p0_6_cellbest_bound.py` re-runs the reproduction and tests
  the per-cell selection rules on toy data.
- **Population.** P0.5's complete-case headline set: 1,907 tasks, 1,701 problems, 10 dimension ×
  source cells. Success means score = 1.0 (S0.1).
- **Cross-fitting.** P0.5's 5-fold split by problem, seeds 0 to 4. Seed 0 is the gate seed (S0.8).
  The best single model is chosen on the training folds. It is Opus in every fold of every seed.
- **Candidates.** 92 per mode: direct dispatch to each of the 8 models (no verifier charge) and
  the 84 cost-ordered 2- and 3-rung ladders of P0.5 (verifier cost of $10⁻⁶ per visible-test run).
- **Modes.** A ladder rung is accepted when its visible-test verdict accepts. On cells with no
  visible tests, the rung is accepted (`accept`), escalated (`escalate`), or decided by the
  released score (`bound`, the oracle-on-no-signal bound). The released score is read only in
  `bound`.

**Rules for reading the results (approved by the user on 2026-11-19).**

- DP1b judges S0.3(c) on **point estimates**: saving against CellBest > 0 and acceptance gap
  against CellBest ≥ −2 points. (a) and (b) are also judged on point estimates for DP1b. The
  paired 95% cluster-bootstrap CIs over problems (1,000 resamples) are given next to every point
  estimate. They are informational and provide the first power estimate for (c). No policy is
  called failed on a DP1b question because a CI bound fails.
- The S0.3(c) comparator is **CellBest with predicted cells**. True-cell CellBest is a reference.
  Policies that use no oracle are reported as candidate R2 baselines. This document does not
  propose changing the comparator.
- Every CI uses `cluster_boot` with seed 1000 + s, as in P0.5. All policies and P0.5's own routes
  therefore share the same draws per seed, so the comparisons are paired.
- A saving against CellBest that is above 0 on seed 0 is reported as **at the threshold** when it
  falls to 0 or below on another seed, or when its CI lower bound is at or below 0. The margin is
  given in points either way.

### 5.2 Per-cell choice, fitted on the training folds

In each training fold and each cell, the candidates are the 92 columns of that mode:

- **Rule A** picks the cheapest candidate whose training acceptance is at least CellBest's in
  that cell. Ties go to the higher acceptance, then the lower column index.
- **Rule B** picks the highest-acceptance candidate whose training cost is at most CellBest's.
  Ties go to the lower cost, then the lower column index.

CellBest's own column always qualifies, so both rules always have a choice. The script asserts
in every fold and cell that Rule A's training acceptance is at least CellBest's and that Rule B's
training cost is at most CellBest's. The rules are fitted on the training tasks' true cells, as
CellBest is, and each held-out task is routed by its **predicted** cell. In bound mode the
selection uses bound-mode training acceptance, so those rows are an upper bound.

## 6. Is S0.3(c) reachable under the bound? (DP1b criterion, seed 0, point estimates)

**Yes, with fixed ladders and with per-cell choice.**

- **Fixed ladders.** 7 of 84 meet (c) and 6 meet (a), (b) and (c). The best against (c) saves
  24.7% against CellBest at a gap of −0.6, but it is an in-sample pick of 84 (CI: [20.5, 28.6],
  [−2.9, +1.7]).
- **Per-cell choice, Rule A.** Saving 36.6% against CellBest at a gap of +1.6 (CI: [33.6, 39.5],
  [−0.3, +3.4]). It also meets (a) and (b): 48.1% and +4.5 against Opus.
- **Per-cell choice, Rule B.** Saving 16.4% against CellBest at a gap of +8.1 (CI: [12.4, 20.5],
  [+6.8, +9.4]). It also meets (a) and (b): 31.6% and +11.0 against Opus.

The verdict is the same on all five seeds. The per-cell family is fitted in the training folds,
so it is the cleaner evidence. The largest point saving against CellBest at gap ≥ −2 is Rule A in
the bound, at 36.6% (36.1% to 38.5% across seeds).

**Informational, CI lower bounds.** In the bound, Rule A and Rule B also pass (c) on CI lower
bounds (saving lower bound > 0, gap lower bound ≥ −2). The best fixed ladder does not: its gap
lower bound is −2.9. This does not change the DP1b answer.

**The gap a learned policy must close against CellBest.** Among fixed ladders with a point saving
above 0 against CellBest, the best gap is −16.0 points with no-signal cells accepted and −16.8
escalated. The counterpart against Opus in P0.5 was −13.1 at ≥ 20% saving. Per-cell choice
without an oracle (§7) already reaches −0.3 and +0.3 at a positive saving, so the 16-point figure
applies to fixed ladders only.

## 7. Per-cell choice without an oracle: candidate R2 baseline

Rules A and B in the accept and escalate modes use the real visible-test verdicts and no released
score. They are buildable lookup-plus-cascade policies, so they are reported here as **candidate
R2 baselines**. This does not change the S0.3(c) comparator, which stays CellBest with predicted
cells.

Measured on seed 0 (predicted cells), the four no-oracle policies:

- meet (c) on point estimates, at 1.7% to 3.3% saving against CellBest and gaps of −0.3 to +0.3;
- pass (c) on CI lower bounds as well, except Rule B accepted, whose saving lower bound is −0.03;
- have a saving against Opus of 19.6% to 20.9%. The CI lower bound is below 20% for all four
  (17.3% to 18.8%).

## 8. Power for (c)

Half-widths of the paired 95% CI against CellBest with predicted cells on seed 0
(`ci_informational`):

| Policy | Gap half-width, points | Saving half-width, points |
|---|---|---|
| Per-cell, accept or escalate, predicted cells | 0.34 to 0.67 | 1.4 to 1.8 |
| Per-cell, oracle bound, predicted cells | 1.26 (B) and 1.83 (A) | 4.1 (B) and 2.9 (A) |
| Best fixed ladder in the bound | 2.33 | 4.0 |
| Fixed ladders with best gap at saving > 0 (accept, escalate, bound) | 2.43, 2.54, 1.46 | n/a |

The half-width grows with how far a policy's routing departs from CellBest's. A policy that
changes few cells is measured against CellBest to about ±0.5 points. One that changes the model or
ladder in most cells is measured to about ±1.3 to ±2.3 points. On the S0.3 gate, which uses CI
lower bounds, a router that departs from CellBest as much as the bound-mode per-cell policies
would need a point gap of about −0.2 to −0.7 or better to keep the gap lower bound at −2. These
figures come from the bound policies and are a first estimate only. S3.7 recomputes power on the
final population.

## 9. Caveats

- **Templates reveal the source.** The prompt templates largely identify the source benchmark.
  cruxeval and ds1000 are separable from their first line, and HumanEval and MBPP prompts differ
  by wording. The predicted cell is therefore close to the source label for most tasks. §2 of the
  plan rules out the source as a router feature, but here it enters through the prompt text. This
  does not change the comparator.
- **Identical prompts under several dimensions.** 63 of the 67 small-bcb tasks share their exact
  prompt with a task in another cell. Whether one model's outcome differs between such twins was
  not checked here.
- **Ladder cost order.** The 84 ladders take their cost order from the whole population, P0.5's
  leftover simplification. Rules A and B inherit it through the candidate columns.
- **In-sample ladder pick.** The best fixed ladder against (c) is the best of 84 on the scored
  folds. Only the per-cell family is fitted out of sample.
- **Small cells.** algorithm\|bcb, code_completion\|bcb and data_science\|bcb have 29, 26 and 12
  tasks, so each training fold sees about 10 to 23 of them. Their per-cell choices change from fold
  to fold (`seed0.fold_picks`).
- **Classifier fitted on the complete case only.** Like P0.5's routes, it is fitted on the
  training folds of the 1,907 tasks, not on the full S0.6 pool (P0.5 open question 3).
- **The bound reads the released score** on cells with no visible tests (80% of the set). Rows
  marked "oracle bound" are upper bounds, not achievable policies.

## 11. Open questions

1. **The comparator's own saving.** CellBest with predicted cells saves 18.2% to 19.0% against
   Opus on point estimates, below S0.3(a)'s 20%. The cause is structural: identical prompts
   across the bcb cells. The comparator was fixed before these results and stays as it is. This
   is recorded so that S0.3's three conditions are read together.
2. **R2 reporting.** Should the no-oracle per-cell policies (Rules A and B, accept and escalate)
   be added to the list of baselines that R2 always reports? They are buildable and meet (c) on
   point estimates. This is a reporting question, not a comparator change.
3. **"At the threshold".** The basis used here is a positive seed-0 saving that falls to 0 or
   below on another seed, or whose CI lower bound is at or below 0. The user may prefer a fixed
   margin in points.
